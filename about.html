About Attendance Management System

The Attendance Management System is a web-based application developed to simplify the process of managing student attendance. It provides separate dashboards for Admin, Teachers, and Students, making attendance recording fast, secure, and efficient.

Admin

Manage teachers, students, and monitor the complete system.

Teacher

Take daily attendance, manage classes, and generate attendance reports easily.

Student

View attendance records, attendance percentage, and download attendance reports.

Our Mission

Our goal is to replace manual attendance registers with a digital system that saves time, improves accuracy, and provides instant attendance reports for educational institutions.
